# Public Tracking Promotion Reference

The existing New World Cargo tracking page at `https://app.newworldcargo.com/en/shipments/tracking` was reviewed on 23 August 2026. It presents a central tracking form with surrounding promotional blocks for **Sea Freight Special**, **Warehouse Solutions**, and an app-download message.

The replacement portal deliberately keeps tracking first. Its promotional content is placed **below** the tracking form and any tracking result, uses two compact service cards rather than side-rail advertisements, and links to the official New World Cargo website. This preserves a mobile-first, task-focused experience while retaining service discovery.
